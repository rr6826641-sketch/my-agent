## 📌 Design Rules (guardrails)

- **No planet / globe shape** — core is a compact crystalline neural sphere, never a world map
- **No humanoid robot** — pure machine-intelligence abstraction
- **No text / no logo** — identity comes from form + light, not glyphs
- **No aggression** — intimidating through precision, symmetry and technology
- **Palette**: black + graphite + blue-white (#7FD4FF family) accents
- **Mood**: intelligence, control, command-center, cybersecurity
